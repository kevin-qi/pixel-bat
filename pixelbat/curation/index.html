Words, in panel order

sort
a Kilosort output folder. Open… takes any folder: a prepared sort opens, another folder is listed. Everything of a sort's curation is in its curation folder: the prepared cache, your decisions (saved as you make them) and the export.
chips and shading
dashed chip: Bombcell's proposal; filled ✓ chip: your label, which also shades the unit's text and puts a bar in its colour at its left. Selected: blue outline and blue bar at the right. Five squares: Bombcell's label on 5 draws of 500 spikes (the outlined first one is the proposal).
number columns
foreign %: estimated share of the unit's spikes from other cells (pairs 0.25–1.25 ms apart against what its own firing 10–50 ms away predicts; red over 20%, where the proposal turns mua). KS ratio: Kilosort's contamination ratio (amber from 0.2). short ISI %: intervals of 0.25–1.25 ms (amber over 0.5%). µV: amplitude (of a merged unit: the spike-weighted mean of its parts). present %: minutes with spikes. Blank: too few spikes to test.
decision strip
Kilosort: what it would have done (the sort ran unmerged), and its template similarity (of the least similar pair). Bombcell: its label for the merged unit. this tool: merge (duplicate spikes, or every pair passes the refractory test), keep apart (a pair fails, or the merged unit would be over 20% foreign), or untestable (under 3 spikes expected within 1 ms, or a dip that is not significant). you: your decision; an undecided group is exported split. Groups under 500 spikes in all are not listed.
cross-correlogram
spike pairs at each lag as a multiple of chance (1× = independent units), on a log axis shared by the group's pairs. Kilosort 4's refractory test is drawn in it: the 1 ms bins it counts (blue, with their level) must lie under the pass line (red dashed: its limit times the flank level, black). The verdict is in the corner; the pair that decides the group's reading is framed.
footprint, spikes per 5 min
peak-to-peak amplitude per channel against depth, each unit over its own largest (r: their correlation, on the channels both have data on); spike counts over the session.
foreign spikes
the foreign % of each unit and of the merge, against the 20% line, with the pairs seen and expected 0.25–1.25 ms apart.
possible merges
pairs no one proposed: waveforms alike across channels (r ≥ 0.85) and an empty centre or turn-taking. A pair kept apart stays in place, folded to its header.
uncertain, few spikes, non-somatic
Bombcell's label differs between draws; under 500 spikes; a tag for an axonal-looking waveform that does not change the proposal.
How the numbers are computed

Refractory test: Kilosort 4's cross-refractory test on the two units' 1 ms cross-correlogram (larger unit first): the least filled window of ±1 to ±10 bins around zero against the flank level (10–50 ms or the far shoulders); it passes when that ratio is under the sort's limit and the dip is significant (p < 0.05). A merge drops duplicates by Kilosort's rule. Bombcell's label of a merged unit uses its spike numbers with the shape of its largest member.

Amplitude floor: with one unmerged unit selected, click in its amplitude plot to try a floor; t applies it, shift+T removes it. The spikes below are exported as a unit of their own, labelled noise.

Plots: rows of a group share waveform scale (set by the units not proposed noise) and amplitude axis; an amplitude axis breaks when its top is over 3 times the largest 99.5th percentile of those units. Rate maps: GridBat's, 0.1 m bins, smoothed 1.2 bins, each coloured 0 to its 99th percentile.
